🌍 Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes Airbnb performance across major global cities.
The project explores listing growth, pricing patterns, market concentration, customer engagement, host trust, and seasonal travel trends.

This dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly understand how Airbnb markets perform across different cities and time periods.

📌 Project Overview

The Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard provides a data-driven analysis of Airbnb listings and reviews across multiple international cities.

It helps answer important analytical questions such as:

• Which cities dominate the Airbnb marketplace?
• How have listings grown over time?
• What accommodation types are most expensive?
• How frequently do customers leave reviews?
• Are hosts verified and trustworthy?
• What seasonal patterns exist in travel demand?

The dashboard converts raw Airbnb data into interactive visual insights that support better business and tourism decisions.

⭐ Dashboard Highlights
Business Problem

Airbnb operates in hundreds of cities and generates huge volumes of listing and review data. However, analyzing this data manually is difficult.

Stakeholders often struggle to quickly understand:

• Which cities dominate Airbnb’s market share
• How pricing varies across room types
• What drives customer engagement
• Whether hosts are trustworthy
• How travel demand changes across seasons

Without clear visualizations, extracting insights from raw data becomes challenging.

🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this project is to build a comprehensive analytics dashboard that allows users to:

• Explore Airbnb market performance globally
• Identify high-performing cities
• Compare pricing across accommodation types
• Analyze customer review patterns
• Evaluate host trust and verification levels
• Discover seasonal travel trends

📊 Key Dashboard Insights
📈 Listings Growth Over Time

The dashboard visualizes Airbnb listing growth from 2008 to 2020.

Key Insights:

• Rapid expansion occurred between 2012 and 2015.
• 2015 recorded the highest number of new listings.
• Growth slowed during 2016–2017 due to regulatory restrictions.
• Another growth phase began in 2018 before the COVID-19 slowdown in 2020.

🌎 Market Share by City

A Pareto-style chart reveals the distribution of listings across cities.

Key Insights:

• Paris, New York City, and Sydney account for nearly 48% of total listings and reviews.
• Paris leads the market in both listings and customer engagement.

This indicates that Airbnb demand is heavily concentrated in major tourist destinations.

💰 Average Price by Room Type

The dashboard compares pricing across accommodation types:

Room Type	Avg Price
Hotel Room	$800
Entire Place	$673
Shared Room	$580
Private Room	$462

Insight:
Hotel rooms are the most expensive option, while private rooms offer the most affordable stays.

⭐ City Ratings Analysis

A ratings heatmap compares cities across multiple metrics:

• Accuracy
• Check-in
• Cleanliness
• Communication
• Location
• Value

Key Insights:

• Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro receive the highest overall ratings.
• Hong Kong and Istanbul score lower in comparison.
• Cleanliness and value tend to receive lower scores across cities.

📝 Review Frequency Insights

Customer review behavior shows a strong skew toward one-time reviewers.

Key Insights:

• Most customers write only one review.
• 98.8% of users leave three reviews or fewer.
• One extreme case shows a reviewer leaving 283 reviews.

This suggests limited repeat review behavior among users.

📅 Seasonality of Reviews

Seasonal patterns reveal tourism cycles across cities.

Key Insights:

• Paris and Rome dominate review share from April to August, reflecting peak European tourism.
• New York experiences increased reviews in November and December, likely driven by holiday travel.

🔐 Host Trust & Verification

The dashboard evaluates trust based on identity verification and profile pictures.

Key Insights:

• Over two-thirds of hosts are fully verified with profile pictures.
• Very few hosts lack both identity verification and profile images.

This indicates strong trust signals across the platform.
